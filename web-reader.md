# Web reader

This file is how the reader behaves and how it is drawn.

The page background stays white, `--bg-page` (`#FFFFFF`). A gray fill that sits on that white is background elevated, `--bg-elevated` (`#F8F9FA`). A gray fill that sits on that gray is surface default, `--surface-default` (`#F1F3F4`). Lines and dividers stay `#F1F3F4`.

## Lesson text

- Words match letters and numbers. Punctuation stays outside the word frame.
- Each word shows the learner’s saved status: **Known**, a **LingQ** at levels 1–4, or **New** (blue).
- Multiple glosses render joined with ` ; `. A saved meaning replaces the glossary for that word.
- LingQ and blue highlights have no border until that word is open in a popup. Hover does not add one. An open LingQ, including Learned, takes a `#dea201` border. An open blue word takes `--fg-secondary`. An open Known or Ignored word takes `--fg-muted`.
- Phrase highlights use a stroke that does not change text layout.
- The reading column is 1000px wide and centered, with no horizontal padding, so the paragraph box is 1000px. Body text defaults to 18px regular with a 36px line height. Paragraphs stay one line apart. Each word has 1px of vertical padding and none on the sides. Page, scroll, sentence, and the theater caption use this same lesson text. The English line, in sentence mode and in theater, is 16px regular, `#49525B` in sentence mode, with a line height of 1.25. Blue words are `#C0E0FF`. LingQ 1 is `#FFE787`, LingQ 2 is `#FFF1BF`, and LingQ 3 is `#FFF7D8`. Those same fills are used on the status buttons. Learned, Known, and Ignored words have no fill.

## Status

| Status | Highlight |
| --- | --- |
| Ignored | Ignored |
| New | LingQ 1 |
| Recognized | LingQ 2 |
| Familiar | LingQ 3 |
| Learned | LingQ 4 |
| Known | Known |

A blue word’s label is already “New”, but it is not a LingQ until something below creates one.

Applying the status the word or phrase already has leaves it unchanged.

## Opening a word

- **Click** opens the small widget and plays the word. The small widget has no play control. Clicking that same word again plays it again and leaves the popup open. A saved phrase works the same way.
- **Click a blue word** creates a LingQ 1, then opens the small widget and plays the word, while Auto LingQ creation is on. While that setting is off, the word stays blue. The small widget opens with the blue plus. Clicking the plus saves it as a LingQ 1, using the top meaning, and closes the popup.
- **Click another word** opens that word’s popup.
- **Long-press (~480ms)** opens the medium widget. While Auto LingQ creation is on, a blue word first becomes a LingQ 1, the same as a click, so the card opens in the LingQ state. While it is off, the word stays blue and the card opens in that blue state. Movement over 8px cancels the press. The click that follows a long-press is ignored.
- Scrolling the word out of view closes the small and medium widgets.
- Clicking outside the popup closes it. Escape closes it too.

## Small widget

Anchored 8px above the word, inside the text column. Flips below if it would leave the column, and clamps horizontally.

- Meaning wraps (max width ~300px). No ellipsis. Status chip and chevron stay vertically centered. Short meanings stay at least 50px tall. While the word is still blue, that status chip is the blue plus. Clicking it saves the word as a LingQ 1, with its top meaning, and closes the popup.
- Status chip morphs into a horizontal status bar (50px tall). Back chevron returns to the meaning.
- Choosing a status applies it and closes the widget. Choosing the current status only closes it.
- Forward chevron opens the large widget. It matches a large-widget section chevron in size, with 9px padding and a 16px icon, but it has no fill. Hover fills it with the elevated grey. It is a pill, and so is the back chevron. While the side panel is open, that chevron is hidden. The meaning row has nothing left to open.

## Medium widget

Long-press card. It is not clipped by the text area, so it can extend past that box, and it shifts to stay on screen.

**LingQ** (saved word or saved phrase): the meaning in the header, 16px regular with a 20px line. Play sits on the right of the status button, then the chevron. It is a pill with no fill until hover, like the chevron, and it plays the term. Then one row with Phrase, From here, a 30px divider, and the tags. While Show the full status bar is on, the full status bar sits along the bottom, and Ignore and Known are not repeated on that row. While it is off, Ignore and Known lead that row as icon buttons. The chevron is hidden while the side panel is open.

**Blue word** (Auto LingQ creation off): the same header as a LingQ. Between that header and the actions row, two suggested meanings use the large widget’s meanings-page rows: the elevated grey card, the meaning, and a blue plus. There are always two, even when the word has more, so the limit is visible before the large widget. A meaning is never the original term. Choosing one saves the word as a LingQ 1 with that meaning and closes the popup. Show the full status bar does not apply while the word is still new: there is no status bar and no status button until a meaning saves it as a LingQ. The suggested meanings are how this card saves the word. The blue plus stays on the small popup.

**New** (unsaved valid phrase): white header “Suggested Meanings” at 14px regular. Play sits to the right of the status button, then the chevron, with no fill until hover, and it plays the phrase. Up to 2 glosses with a plus and the same inset dividers, then the dictionary row directly under the meanings. The actions and tags row stays at the bottom. While Show the full status bar is on, that bar is the bottommost element. The status control is the blue plus. Choosing a suggested meaning, or the plus, saves the phrase as a LingQ 1 and closes the popup.

- Tag and dictionary chips match: radius 4, `#F8F9FA`, 12px regular, `#49525B`. The add-tag button is a 16px icon with 9px padding, 4px from the chips. The dictionary button is the same size on a white fill. Dictionaries sit directly under the meanings. The actions and tags row stays at the bottom, and the full status bar is the bottommost element when it is on. Meaning rows use the same inset divider as the large widget. The add-tag button adds a tag. A dictionary chip opens that dictionary. The dictionary button opens the dictionaries page.
- Tag and dictionary rows scroll by swipe or click-and-drag. A drag does not fire the button. The tags row scrolls as one piece: Phrase, From here, the divider, add tag, and the tag chips move together. While the full status bar is off, Ignore and Known scroll with them at the front.
- Phrase and From here sit on the tags row in both states, before a 30px divider. Phrase uses Lucide TextCursorInput. From here uses Lucide Play. Both match the same action button. While the full status bar is off, Ignore and Known sit in front of them on that same button, with Lucide EyeOff and Check and no label.
- The status bar is on both the LingQ and new-word cards while Show the full status bar is on. That switch starts on. Choosing a status on a saved word updates it and leaves the widget open. Choosing a suggested meaning on an unsaved phrase, or its plus, saves the phrase as a LingQ 1 and closes the popup. While the switch is off, the status bar is removed. Ignore and Known lead the actions row, then Phrase, From here, and the tags stay as they are. Ignore marks the term Ignored. Known marks it Known. On an unsaved phrase, either one saves that status and closes the popup. The status button sits 8px to the left of Play, and Play sits 8px to the left of the chevron. The status button shows the word’s status, or the blue plus on an unsaved phrase. Clicking it opens the same vertical status menu as a status button in the side-panel vocabulary list. The menu stays 8px to the side of the button, and its middle lines up with the middle of the button. The current status is filled. Choosing one applies it the same way the bar does and closes the menu. The large widget and the small widget keep their own status controls.
- Forward chevron opens the large widget. It matches a large-widget section chevron in size and is a pill, with no fill until hover, which uses the elevated grey. While the side panel is open, that chevron is hidden.
- **Phrase** starts phrase selection. **From here** closes the widget, opens the player, and starts playback from the sentence this term is in. A lesson with video opens the video. That sentence, or its paragraph while the layout is By paragraphs, is the highlighted block. Page mode opens the page that shows this term. Scroll mode brings that term into view. Sentence mode shows that sentence.

## Large widget

Centered in the lesson column, 400×600 (shrinks if the column is shorter). Not anchored to the word.

- Shows the term, meanings, the full sentence, and its English translation. Original sentence is 16px regular, not italic, and clamps to 2 lines. Translation is 14px regular and does not clamp. The sentence card is at least 128px tall. German transliteration is hidden.
- Notes text is 16px regular, matching the meaning and sentence text. The notes icon is the notebook. A note from Lynx uses the Lynx-marked notebook.
- Header and footer are white. Each section (Meanings, Sentence, Notes) keeps its header outside a `#F8F9FA` card, radius 12, with 12px padding on the left and right.
- Left of Play, the header shows how many coins the term is worth. A 24px coin, then 4px, then a number from 1 to 4. The number is 14px medium, `#49525B`, with an 18px line height. Each term is assigned one number, and that number stays with the term. The coin sits 12px left of Play. It is on the floating card, in the default side panel, and on the tabbed Term tab. The Dictionaries page header does not show it.
- Section labels are 14px regular, `#49525B`, 18px line height. Icons and chevrons are 16px. Lucide strokes are 1.33. The Sentence icon is the custom image at 16px.
- The reader's default grey buttons, including these section chevrons, are `#F8F9FA`. The section chevrons are pills. Borders and the meaning dividers are `#F1F3F4`.
- Meaning dividers are inset from the left, in line with the text, and run to the right edge.
- Tags are not in the term header. They sit under the meanings. Dictionaries are not on the main page. They stay on the meanings page.
- **New** variant (unsaved valid phrase): up to 3 suggested meanings with a plus, then the tags. Dictionaries are not on this page.
- A saved word with no meanings left shows up to three suggested meanings in that card, with an Add meaning row under them. Choosing a suggestion shows Added, then saves it on the word. Saving a custom meaning from that row closes the widget. With no saved meaning, the status bar has nothing selected.
- Saving a meaning creates a LingQ 1 and **keeps the widget open**, switching it to the saved form.
- Footer status bar is always the full bar, including while Auto LingQ creation is off and the word is still blue. Nothing is selected until the word or phrase is saved. Choosing a status updates the word or saved phrase and leaves the widget open. On an unsaved phrase it saves the phrase as a LingQ with that status and the first suggested meaning, and leaves the widget open. The Show the full status bar setting does not change this bar.
- Click outside or Escape closes the floating card. In the side panel, a click outside leaves the word there. Escape, and the X, return the default panel to the review underneath: the vocabulary list, or the activity in progress. On the Dictionaries page, Escape goes back to the page that opened it.
- The floating card has no side-panel button. In the default panel and the tabbed panel, that button stays in the lesson header.
- The Meanings chevron opens the meanings page: saved meanings (with the same dividers), an Add meaning row, tags, then the dictionary row, then Suggested Meanings with a locale flag and a report button. Each suggestion shows a blue plus, and that plus is a pill. Hover fills that plus. Clicking anywhere on the row shows Added, then the meaning joins the saved list above. Back returns to the default page. The Add meaning row keeps the grey card when it is the only row. Hovering a saved meaning reveals remove. Clicking it edits the text. The check or Enter saves it: the check slides left and the row briefly reads Saved. Remove slides the X left and briefly reads Removed, then the meaning is gone. The Add meaning plus stays blue, is a pill, and the label turns blue on hover. Clicking it starts a blank meaning. The same edit states are on the default page’s saved meanings.
- The Sentence chevron opens the sentence page: the current sentence, a horizontal row of the words in that sentence, and Past Encounters. Original sentences on this page, including past encounters, use the same 16px regular style and are not truncated. Translations are 14px regular. A saved word lists up to three other lesson sentences that contain it. A new word, or a word with no earlier sentence, shows the first-encounter note. Back returns to the default page.
- The dictionary icon opens the Dictionaries page: Preferred lists the current dictionaries, All Resources lists more with a plus, and the English flag sits on that heading. Section labels stay 14px regular. The status bar is hidden on this page. Back returns to the page the icon was on. Adding a resource adds it to Preferred.
- Play plays the term. Copy copies it. Generate asks Lynx for a note. The notes field saves the note. Adding and removing tags edits them. A dictionary chip opens that dictionary. The report button reports that suggestion.

## Select phrase

1. **Phrase** closes the medium widget, doubles the start word’s stroke, and shows “Select another word to create a phrase.” at the bottom center of the lesson text.
2. The next word click selects the inclusive span. Clicking the start word again leaves the selection unchanged.
3. A phrase is valid when it is 2–9 words, in one paragraph, with no `.` `!` `?` strictly between the ends. Trailing punctuation after the last word is part of the phrase text.
4. **Valid:** the span highlights as a new word, and the medium widget opens in the new-word state: the blue plus, and two suggested meanings. Choosing either meaning, or the plus, saves the phrase as a LingQ 1 and closes the popup. The plus uses the first suggested meaning.
5. **Invalid** (over 9 words, a sentence break, or a cross-paragraph span): grey highlight and an error card where the medium widget would be. The card shows the phrase text, **Cancel**, and **Google Translate** (`sl=de`, `tl=en`).
6. Clicking away from an unsaved phrase cancels it. Nothing is saved.
7. Click a saved phrase to open the small widget. Long-press opens the medium widget. Status changes apply to the phrase.

Known phrase meanings come from a small glossary. Anything else falls back to word-by-word glosses.

## Blue terms

A blue term is passed when the reader moves on without it being tapped. Turning the page passes the blue terms on the page that was left. Moving to another sentence passes the blue terms in the sentence that was left. In scroll mode, a blue term is passed once it scrolls out through the top. Exit and Finish lesson also pass the blue terms still in view, because leaving the lesson moves on from them. Tapping a blue term removes it from the passed set. Refreshing the page clears the passed terms and Auto-move and starts from Auto-move off. That reset is only for this prototype, so the lesson can be tried again without closing the page. In the product the passed count is saved with the lesson and is still there the next time that lesson is opened. Auto-move and the remembered status are saved per language.

In the product, Auto-move moves each term as it is passed. Only skipped terms stay collected: pages jumped past from the progress bar or by seeking, pages turned faster than about 500 words per minute, and pages turned by audio or in the background. Blue terms still in view on Finish are moved like a normal page turn. They are not skipped, and they do not open Update Lesson Progress. This prototype cannot tell those skips from ordinary reading, so Auto-move applies the chosen status to every passed term as it is passed, adds the coins, and collects nothing. On with Leave Blue leaves the terms blue, collects nothing, and never asks. While Auto-move is off, terms collect until Exit, Finish, or the stats sheet. Nothing in the lesson header shows the count while you read. On the site-header stats button, each newly passed term turns the coin icon into a `#2E75CD` dot with the accumulated count in its center. The coin amount stays. The number eases up through the words just passed, holds briefly, and the coin icon returns. Passing more before it returns keeps counting from the number on screen. The streak stays. Auto-move does not show it, because those terms are not collected. Exit, Finish, and the stats sheet are where those passed terms are handled.

Exit and Finish lesson open the same Update Lesson Progress dialog in the center of the screen, about 480px wide and only as tall as its content, over a dimmed scrim, when at least one blue term has been passed and Auto-move is off. Exit skips that dialog when Ask when I exit is off. Below 767px that dialog is a bottom sheet: full width, rounded only on top, and only as tall as its content, up to the screen with a gap above it. ## Lynx
